So, what is Web3?

Web3 is a way of building online spaces on shared networks instead of putting everything in the hands of one company. Think of it as an internet where people can participate and have a stake.

Shared foundations

A blockchain is a public record that many computers help maintain. It lets people agree on what happened without one central keeper.

⌘

Your digital key

A wallet lets you sign in, hold digital assets, and prove what's yours across compatible apps. You decide when to connect it.

{ }

Rules in the open

Smart contracts are programs on a blockchain. When someone uses them, they follow rules written into their code.
