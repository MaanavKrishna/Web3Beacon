01 / The basics

First, the
plain English.

Web3 is a loose name for online services built around shared networks and user-held accounts. It’s an idea in progress, not a single new website.

◫ Blockchain
A record kept by a network of computers. On a public blockchain, anyone can check transactions instead of asking one company for its version.
⌘ Wallet
An app that manages your blockchain account and asks you to approve actions. It holds the keys to your account, so its recovery phrase stays private.
{ } Smart contract
A program on a blockchain. When someone uses it, the program follows the rules written in its code.
